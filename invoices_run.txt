=== case 4: collect payment  [menu: invoices_only] ===
you > how do I collect the money for a pending invoice?
[allowed | q: how do I collect the money for a pending invoice? | section: How do I collect payment for a pending invoice?]
bot > 1. Open the **Pending** invoice (from **Search Invoices** or the ticket's **Invoice Number**). **Print** is disabled while the invoice is pending.
2. Scroll to **Payment Methods** and click **Add Payment Method**.
3. In the **Payment Methods** dialog, choose a **Payment Type** (Cash, Visa, Online, Sister Company, Cheque, Instapay, Total Estimated Cost Refund) and enter the **Amount**. Use **+ Add Payment Method** to split the payment across types.
4. Check that **Remaining** is **0.00**, then click **Collect**.
5. *"Invoice collected successfully."* appears. The invoice becomes **Collected** and, for a ticket invoice, the ticket becomes **Paid**.

3. Click Void Request, or Cancel.

=== case 6: void directly  [menu: invoices_only] ===
you > how do I void an invoice immediately without a request?
[allowed | q: how do I void an invoice immediately without a request? | section: How do I void an invoice directly?]
bot > 1. Open the invoice and click **Void Invoice**.
2. Read *"Are you sure you want to void this invoice? This action cannot be undone."* and type the reason.
3. Click **Void Invoice**, or **Cancel**.
4. *"Invoice voided successfully."* appears; status becomes **Void** (verified). Cannot be undone.

=== case 7: refund request  [menu: invoices_only] ===
you > how do I submit a refund request?
[allowed | q: how do I submit a refund request? | section: How do I request a refund for an invoice?]
bot > 1. Go to Invoices → Refund.
2. Find the invoice: filter by Invoice Number, Invoice Item Number, Status, ERP Reference, ERP Refund Reference, Date From, Date To, and click Search.
3. In the invoice's row, click Refund Request (shown for invoices that have not been refunded yet).
4. The dialog asks "Are you sure you want to submit a refund request for this invoice?" — type the reason in Enter the reason for requesting this invoice refund....
5. Click Refund Request to submit, or Cancel.
6. The row's Refund Status becomes Refund Requested, with your name in Refund Requested By and your reason in Refund Request Reason.

=== case 8: refund directly  [menu: invoices_only] ===
you > how do I refund an invoice right away?
[allowed | q: how do I refund an invoice right away? | section: How do I refund an invoice directly?]
bot > 1. Go to Invoices → Refund, find the invoice (see the question above) and click Refund in its row.
2. Read "Are you sure you want to refund this invoice? This action cannot be undone." and type the reason in Enter the reason for refunding this invoice....
3. Click Refund to confirm, or Cancel.
4. The row's Refund Status becomes Refunded, with your name in Refunded By and your reason in Refund Reason.
⚠️ A direct refund cannot be undone. If the refund should be reviewed first, use Refund Request instead.

=== case 10: end of day  [menu: invoices_only] ===
you > how do I settle my end of day?
[allowed | q: How do I settle my end of day? | section: How do I review my End of Day?]
bot > 1. Go to **Invoices → End Of Day**.
2. Pick the **Date** and click **Search**. The page shows the **Employee**, **Cash Payments** totals (Total Collected, Total Voided, Void Requested, Total Refunded, Partially Refunded, Refund Requested, Net Balance), **Payment Totals** (Total Pending), and the invoice list.
3. Click **Settle**.
4. The dialog **Confirm Settlement** asks: *"Are you sure you want to settle the end of day? This action cannot be undone."* Click **Settle** to confirm, or **Cancel**.
5. The toast *"Settlement completed successfully."* appears. Settled (Collected/Void) invoices leave the list; **Pending** invoices stay, so **Settle** is still shown.
⚠️ Settlement cannot be undone. Check the totals before you confirm.
